How it works

  1. Connect Lichess. Log in with Lichess (or paste a token). Lichess requires this to download games.
  2. Download. Your whole history streams straight from Lichess into this browser.
  3. Analyse. Stockfish 19 checks every position on your device, newest games first. Stop and resume any time.
